A bounded path from People to your workspace.

Credit Monitor reads the row marked (you) in Lovable Settings > People, saves the resulting usage snapshot in chrome.storage.local, and renders the monitor in your workspace.

  1. PeopleLovable is the source of truth.
  2. Local storageUsage stays in your browser.
  3. WorkspaceThe monitor makes it visible while you work.

See it inside the workspace.

Use the real Credit Monitor controls inside this simulated workspace. All values are fictional and the demo never connects to Lovable.

Useful signal. Minimal interference.

Verified at the source

Passive sync reads only the (you) row in Settings > People. Manual Sync reuses that source only when you request a fresh value.

Stays in your browser

Usage snapshots, sync state and preferences remain in chrome.storage.local—with no project server, separate account or telemetry.

Fits the workspace

Full, Compact, Minimal and Ring views keep edge-aware placement, while motion feedback follows your reduced-motion preference.

No analytics.
No extension backend.

Credit Monitor is intentionally small in scope. It reads the Lovable People view, stores the result in your browser and renders the monitor locally.

  • No fetch/XHR interception
  • No route automation or synthetic clicks
  • No keyboard-event injection
  • No project-owned telemetry
